To get the best result from an AI design tool (like v0.dev, Midjourney, or a specialized UI generator) or to hand off to a developer, you need a prompt that combines **Information Architecture**, **Psychological Triggers**, and **Technical Requirements**.

Here is a comprehensive prompt you can use.

---

### **The "AmalTrack V2" Redesign Prompt**

**Role:** You are a World-Class Senior UI/UX Designer and Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Expert specializing in "Digital Spiritualism" and modern SaaS aesthetics.

**Project:** Redesign the landing page for **AmalTrack**—The GitHub for Good Deeds.

**Design Aesthetic & Vibe:**

- **Theme:** "Modern Islamic Excellence." Use a Deep Emerald and Charcoal Dark Mode base.
- **Accents:** Neon Emerald (#50C878) for activity and Gold (#D4AF37) for achievements.
- **Style:** Glassmorphism, high-depth shadows, and subtle radial "Noor" (light) glows. Avoid generic corporate illustrations; use glowing 3D renders of the "Amal Graph" (heatmap).

---

### **Page Structure & Content Requirements:**

#### **1. Hero Section (The Hook)**

- **Visual:** A floating, perspective-tilted 3D mockup of the AmalTrack Heatmap glowing in the dark.
- **Headline (H1):** "Build Your Legacy. **Track Your Amal.**" (SEO Keyword: Track Good Deeds).
- **Sub-headline:** "The spiritual heatmap for the modern Muslim. Log your worship, visualize your consistency, and transform small actions into a lifelong legacy."
- **Primary CTA:** "Start My Journey — It's Free."
- **Social Proof:** "Join 5,000+ Believers building their spiritual momentum."

#### **2. The "Spiritual Gap" Section (Pain Points - NEW)**

- **Layout:** A 2-column "Problem vs. Solution" layout or a "Mirror" effect.
- **Concept:** Speak to the user’s heart.
- **Content:**
  - _The Pain:_ "The angels record every deed, but in the noise of Dunya, we often lose sight of our own progress. We intend to pray, we intend to give, but forgetfulness wins."
  - _The Solution:_ "AmalTrack makes the invisible record visible. It acts as a digital mirror for your soul, helping you bridge the gap between your intentions and your actions."
- **UI Detail:** Use a slightly desaturated/blurry background for the "Pain" and a sharp, glowing Emerald border for the "Solution."

#### **3. The "Amal Graph" Section (The Hero Visual)**

- **Headline (H2):** "Visibility Breeds Consistency." (SEO: Islamic Habit Tracker).
- **Content:** "The same dopamine loop that drives world-class developers is now repurposed for your Akhirah. Watch your green dots grow day by day."
- **Features to Highlight:** 365-day Heatmap, Streak Multipliers, and Consistency Analytics.

#### **4. The Library of Light (SEO Engine)**

- **Headline (H2):** "80+ Verified Deeds from Quran & Sunnah." (SEO: Islamic Daily Adhkar/Sunnah Checklist).
- **Content:** "Never wonder what to do next. Access a curated library of Sunnah prayers, Adhkar, and Sadaqah, all backed by authentic references."
- **UI Style:** Dark glassmorphic cards with "Verified" badges.

#### **5. Accountability & Community (The Portfolio)**

- **Headline (H2):** "A Spiritual Portfolio to Inspire."
- **Content:** "Share your progress without compromising your sincerity. Use your public profile to inspire your family and friends to join the race for good."
- **Visual:** Mobile phone mockup showing the `@username` profile.

---

### **SEO & Technical Constraints:**

- **Hierarchy:** Use only one `H1`. Use `H2` for all section titles. Use `aria-labels` for all interactive dots.
- **Copywriting Tone:** Sophisticated, spiritually grounding, yet high-tech. Use words like "Legacy," "Ummah," "Sincerity," and "Momentum."
- **Final Footer:** Include a "Niyyah" (Intention) statement and a link to the "Ramadan Mode" feature for seasonal SEO.

---

### **Instructions for Layout:**

"Ensure the transitions between sections feel organic, using 'wave' or 'aurora' separators rather than straight lines. The primary goal is to make the user feel like they are entering a calm, focused digital space for their worship."
